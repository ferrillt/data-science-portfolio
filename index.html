About Me

I am an enterprise data architect and technical leader with approximately 25 years of experience in enterprise IT and Department of Defense environments. My background includes database development, data integration, systems engineering, and technical program leadership. In my role as a Senior Principal Engineer and Enterprise Data Architect, I help guide data platform architecture, modernization, and the integration of information used for analytics and AI initiatives.

My data science work builds on that experience through predictive modeling, exploratory analysis, and data visualization. The projects in this portfolio examine questions in housing, education, consumer behavior, and public policy. I focus on preparing reliable data, evaluating results against appropriate baselines, explaining limitations, and communicating findings clearly to people who need to make decisions.

My interests include data architecture, data engineering, predictive analytics, and the responsible application of machine learning. I am completing a Master of Science in Data Science and hold a Master of Science in Management Information Systems and IT Project Management.

Time-Series Forecasting

Hays County Real Estate Analysis

Can housing inventory, mortgage rates, and seasonal patterns help forecast next month's median days on market? This project combines county housing data with mortgage-rate information and compares predictive models with historical baselines.

**Course:** DSC 680 — Applied Data Science

The analysis translates forecast results into guidance for real estate planning while explaining the model's limitations.

Tools: Python, pandas, scikit-learn, Matplotlib, Seaborn

Machine Learning & Kaggle

EV Purchase Prediction

This classification project predicts electric vehicle purchase interest using demographic, financial, transportation, charging-access, and attitudinal data.

The analysis compares logistic regression, random forest, and histogram gradient boosting. The final model achieved a validation ROC AUC of 0.9414 and a Kaggle public leaderboard score of 0.94118.

Tools: Python, pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, Matplotlib

Classification & Responsible Analytics

University Dropout Prediction

This project compares classification models for identifying students at risk of withdrawing from a university. It considers how predictive analytics could support earlier student assistance.

The analysis emphasizes recall, model interpretability, fairness evaluation, and the responsible use of predictions in institutional decisions.

Tools: Python, pandas, scikit-learn, XGBoost, Matplotlib
